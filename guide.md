## 3. Google Search Console (GSC) Step-by-Step Setup

### Step 3.1: Add Both Properties in Google Search Console
1. Go to [Google Search Console](https://search.google.com/search-console/).
2. Click **Add Property** → Select **Domain** (left card).
3. Enter `shastraloka.lk` and click **Continue**.
4. Copy the `google-site-verification=...` TXT record provided by Google and paste it into your DNS provider for `shastraloka.lk`. Click **Verify**.
5. Repeat **Add Property** for your Sinhala domain by entering `ශාස්ත්රාලෝක.ලංකා` (or `xn--j1b1a4b2a9d2a.xn--fzc2c9e2c`) and verify via DNS TXT record.

### Step 3.2: Submit Your Sitemap to Google
1. In Google Search Console, select `shastraloka.lk`.
2. In the left sidebar, click **Sitemaps**.
3. Under **Add a new sitemap**, enter:
   ```text
   sitemap.xml
   ```
4. Click **Submit**. Google will immediately discover all your book pages (`/books/...`), search routes (`/search`), and category pages.

### Step 3.3: Force Instant Indexing of Key Pages
1. At the top of Google Search Console, click the **Inspect any URL** bar.
2. Paste these priority URLs one by one and click **Request Indexing**:
   - `https://shastraloka.lk/`
   - `https://shastraloka.lk/search`
   - `https://shastraloka.lk/books/gajaga-wannama-royal-chronicle`
   - `https://shastraloka.lk/books/mahawamsa-great-chronicle`
   - `https://shastraloka.lk/books/visuddhimagga-path-of-purification`
   - `https://shastraloka.lk/books/pansiya-panas-jataka-potha`

### Step 3.4: Enable Google Merchant Center Free Book Listings (LKR Price Tags in Google Search)
1. Go to [Google Merchant Center](https://merchants.google.com/) and link your `shastraloka.lk` Search Console account.
2. Choose **Add products automatically from your website** (Google will automatically read the `Product` + `Offer` `LKR` JSON-LD embedded in your site!).
3. Your books will appear in Google’s **Shopping** tab and organic **Popular Products** carousel with `LKR` prices, `-28% OFF` badges, and `4.98★` ratings.

## 4. Microsoft Bing Webmaster Tools & IndexNow Setup Guide

*(Crucial: Microsoft Bing powers **Bing Search, Yahoo, DuckDuckGo, Microsoft Copilot, and ChatGPT Search**!)*

### Step 4.1: 1-Click Import from Google Search Console
1. Go to [Microsoft Bing Webmaster Tools](https://www.bing.com/webmasters).
2. Sign in with your Microsoft, Google, or Facebook account.
3. Choose **Import from Google Search Console** → Select `shastraloka.lk` and `ශාස්ත්රාලෝක.ලංකා`.
4. Bing will automatically verify your domains and import your `sitemap.xml`!

### Step 4.2: Submit Sitemap & URL Submission in Bing
1. In Bing Webmaster Tools, open **Sitemaps** in the left menu.
2. Verify that `https://shastraloka.lk/sitemap.xml` is listed and status is **Success**.
3. Open **URL Submission** → Click **Submit URLs** and paste your homepage and top book URLs for immediate crawling.

### Step 4.3: Enable IndexNow (Instant Indexing for Bing & ChatGPT)
1. In Bing Webmaster Tools, navigate to **IndexNow** in the left sidebar.
2. Generate your IndexNow API Key (e.g., `a1b2c3d4e5f6.txt`) and place that text file in the `/public/` folder of this project.
3. Whenever you add new books to `src/data/books.ts`, ping:
   ```bash
   curl "https://api.indexnow.org/indexnow?url=https://shastraloka.lk/sitemap.xml&key=YOUR_KEY"
   ```
